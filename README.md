A make.fun entry for **The 3 AM Excuse**: one line, the excuse a hardware
wallet gives for refusing to sign a transaction at 3 AM.

> It is 3 AM. I refuse to sign anything your 9 AM self will regret —
> sleep on it, and if you still want it in daylight, I will be here.

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) writes that line to
`/output/entry.txt`, deterministically.
